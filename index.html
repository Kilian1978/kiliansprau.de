Kilian Sprau is a professor of music theory at the Berlin University of the Arts and a researcher in the field of musical interpretation. He has been active as a collaborative pianist working with singers for many years. His theoretical and practical interests center on the interplay between music and language, with a focus on the repertoire of the 19th and 20th centuries.
